🎂 Brand Profile: Velvet Crumbs

Tagline:
Crafted with precision. Baked with heart.

🧁 About Us

Velvet Crumbs is a premium home-based bakery specializing in custom-designed cakes and artisanal desserts. Founded in 2024 in Karachi, we focus on delivering high-quality, freshly baked products that combine aesthetic design with rich, balanced flavors.

Our goal is simple:
Create cakes that look exceptional and taste even better.
