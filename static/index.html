How it works

schedule

Fresh, not stale

Nothing older than three days. Anything from the last 24 hours comes first.

verified_user

Brand-safe

Tragedies, crime, conflict and adult topics are removed before anything is suggested — plus local sensitivities, like pork and alcohol in Pakistan and the UAE.

link

Traceable

Every suggestion links back to the real search, story, post or video behind it. You decide what gets published.
